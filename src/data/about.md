Niharika Goparaju is a pediatric emergency medicine physician at Dell Children's Medical Center in Austin, Texas, and a member of the faculty at the University of Texas at Austin Dell Medical School.

Her current projects focus on AI literacy: helping clinicians and trainees understand what AI tools can do, where they fall short, and how to use them safely in patient care. Slides and resources from that work are on the [AI literacy page](./ai-literacy/).

## Interests

- Pediatric emergency medicine
- Medical education
- AI literacy in medicine
